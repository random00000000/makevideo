## Tyrannosaurus rex
![Tyrannosaurus rex skeleton museum]()
The tyrant lizard king
> Sixty six million years ago, the largest predator in its world stood around four meters tall at the hips, and weighed more than an elephant. This is Tyrannosaurus rex.

> Chapter one. Discovery.

## Hell Creek, Montana
![Hell Creek Formation Montana badlands]()
The first skeleton, 1902
> In 1902, the fossil hunter Barnum Brown dug the first partial skeleton out of the Hell Creek rocks of Montana. Three years later, Henry Fairfield Osborn gave it a name. Tyrannosaurus rex. The tyrant lizard king.

## Timeline
- 68 Mya: T. rex appears
- 66 Mya: Asteroid impact
- 1902: First skeleton found
- 1990: Sue is discovered
> Its whole story fits on one line. It appears around sixty eight million years ago. It vanishes when the asteroid hits, sixty six million years ago. Its first skeleton is found in 1902. And in 1990, the most complete one ever found: Sue.

## Chapter: Built to kill
![Tyrannosaurus rex skull]()
> Chapter two. Built to kill.

## Bite force, newtons (estimates)
<!-- highlight: T. rex -->
- Lion: 4500 N
- Saltwater crocodile: 16000 N
- T. rex: 35000 N
> Its bite was the strongest of any land animal we know. A lion bites with about four and a half thousand newtons. A saltwater crocodile, the strongest bite alive today, about sixteen thousand. And T. rex? Researchers estimate at least thirty five thousand. Enough to crush bone.

## Spikes, not knives
![Tyrannosaurus rex tooth]()
Teeth up to 30 cm long, root included
> Its teeth were thick, serrated spikes, up to thirty centimeters long including the root. They were made for crushing, not slicing. Fossilized dung shows it swallowed bone.

## Tiny arms
Those tiny arms could curl *200 kilograms*.
> And the famous tiny arms? About a meter long, with only two fingers. Yet one study estimated each arm could curl around two hundred kilograms.

## Chapter: Growing up fast
![Sue Tyrannosaurus Field Museum]()
> Chapter three. Growing up fast.

## Growth spurt
**2 kg** gained per day, in its teenage growth spurt
> A young T. rex grew astonishingly fast. During its teenage growth spurt, it could gain about two kilograms every single day.

## Sue
![Sue Tyrannosaurus skull Field Museum]()
About 90% complete, and around 28 years old
> Sue, found in South Dakota in 1990, is about ninety percent complete. She was around twenty eight years old when she died, one of the oldest T. rex known. Today she stands in the Field Museum in Chicago.

## Impact
Sixty six million years ago, *the sky fell*.
> Sixty six million years ago, an asteroid about ten kilometers wide struck what is now Mexico. In a geological instant, T. rex and every other non-bird dinosaur were gone.

## Still here
The birds outside your window are *living dinosaurs*.
> But dinosaurs did not entirely vanish. The birds outside your window are living dinosaurs. Distant cousins of the tyrant king.
