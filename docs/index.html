Fast, standard video generation for AI agents.

Write a video as Markdown or JSON. makevideo voices it with a local neural voice, times every scene to the real audio, adds captions and transitions, and renders an MP4 — on a plain CPU, faster than it plays.

How it works →

What you get without asking

Narration by default

Every scene is spoken. Same voice on every video. Symbols like 42%, v1.2 and $3M are read correctly.

Premium look

Transitions blended from real frames, kinetic statements, animated charts, split media layouts, bundled fonts.

Hard to get wrong

Lint flags walls of text and unsynced lists before rendering. Errors name the scene and show a working example.

Fast on a CPU

Frame holding, render processes and cached speech: a 74 s video re-renders in ~28 s on a laptop.
